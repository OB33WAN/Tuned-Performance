Car care and booking FAQs

Answers for quotes, photos, guide pricing, weekday and weekend availability, service suitability and how email or WhatsApp enquiries work.

Send enquiryView pricing
Can I send an enquiry by email?

Yes. The homepage, contact page and estimator can send email enquiries through Web3Forms to the connected business inbox.

Can I still use WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons build a message with the details entered in the form. You still need to press send inside WhatsApp for the message to be received.

What photos should I send for repair work?

Send one close-up photo, one wider photo of the full panel, and the vehicle colour or paint code if known.

Is single panel repair and re-spray available?

Yes, single panel repair and re-spray enquiries can be reviewed from photos before quote confirmation.

Are weekends available?

Saturday and Sunday daytime slots run from 10am to 6pm. Saturday and Sunday evening appointments from 6pm to 11.30pm can also be requested, with a £50 evening booking fee.

Can you diagnose warning lights?

OBD diagnostic enquiries can be sent with warning light details, symptoms, vehicle information and location.

Can you code BMW or MINI hidden features?

Yes. BMW and MINI coding is available for supported BimmerCode-compatible vehicles. Send the model, year and feature list first because availability depends on ECU hardware and software support.

Is BMW and MINI coding the same as ECU remapping?

No. Coding changes supported settings and factory personalisation options. Remapping changes engine calibration and is handled separately as an enquiry route.

Are ECU remaps available now?

ECU remapping is positioned as an enquiry route. Register interest with make, model, engine and tuning goal.

What areas are covered?

Core areas include Feltham, Bedfont, Ashford, Sunbury, Hounslow and Kingston, subject to route planning and service type.

Where is Tuned Performance based?

Tuned Performance is based at 105 Swan Road, Feltham, TW13 6PE.
