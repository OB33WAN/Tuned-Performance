Common Brake Questions

Frequently Asked About Mobile Brake Service

Do I need to book a workshop?

No. We come to your home or workplace on weekend appointments. Just confirm your postcode, vehicle model, and preferred weekend slot.

What if my discs need replacing?

We assess discs during the job and provide a clear quote if replacement is needed. You can approve or decline before any disc work starts.

How long does a brake job take?

Pad replacement: 1–1.5 hours. Pad and disc replacement: 2–3 hours. Both axles typically takes 2.5–3 hours total.

Can I supply my own brake pads?

Yes, we accept customer-supplied parts. However, the 12-month wear guarantee only applies to parts we source and supply.

What brake pad options are available?

Economy, mid-range, and performance pads. Pricing varies by brand and spec. We recommend based on your vehicle and driving style.

Which areas do you cover?

Feltham, Bedfont, Ashford, Sunbury, Kingston, Hounslow, Twickenham, Richmond, Hampton, Hampton Hill, Hampton Court, and Brentford.
