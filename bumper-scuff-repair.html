Frequently Asked Questions

How much does bumper scuff repair cost?

Minor scuff spot repairs typically range from £65–£75 (£60 labour + paint). Dent removal with repainting is £90–£145. Full bumper replacement costs £130–£250 depending on sourcing and paint matching. We'll provide an exact quote after photo assessment.

Can you repair my bumper without replacing it?

Yes, we specialize in spot repair for minor scuffs and scratches. If the damage is shallow, we can repair and repaint on-site. For deeper dents or cracks, panel replacement may be more cost-effective and provide a cleaner finish.

How long does bumper repair take?

Spot repairs take 30 minutes to 1 hour. Dent removal and repainting can take 2–3 hours including drying time. Full bumper replacement takes 3–4 hours. Drying time is included in these estimates.

Will the repair match my bumper colour?

Yes, we use your vehicle's colour code (extracted from your car's VIN or door jamb) to source exact paint matches. Our paint supplier provides colour-coded formulas for precise matching. Clear coat protection ensures colour longevity.

Is bumper repair covered by insurance?

Many insurance policies cover bumper damage under comprehensive or collision coverage. We can provide a detailed quote for your insurance claim. No warranty issues as all work is standard repair practice.

Do you cover all vehicle makes and models?

Yes, we repair bumpers on all cars, vans, and light commercial vehicles. Colour code extraction and bumper sourcing available for all mainstream makes.
