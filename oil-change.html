Oil Change Questions

Frequently Asked About Oil Changes

How often should I change my oil?

Typically every 10,000 miles or 12 months for modern vehicles (check your handbook for your specific schedule). Older vehicles may need 5,000-6,000 mile intervals.

What oil grade does my car need?

Check your vehicle handbook or door jamb sticker. We advise on the correct grade and can source it for you. Common grades are 5W-30 and 10W-40.

Can you change oil on my diesel car?

Yes. Diesel engines often need different oil specifications. We source the correct diesel-specific oil for your vehicle.

Can I supply my own oil?

Yes. If you provide your own oil and filter, we can fit them for £45 labour. However, we recommend using quality oil sourced by us for warranty protection.

Is new filter always included?

Yes. A new filter is fitted with every oil change. Filter disposal is included.

Do you cover all 12 areas?

Yes. Oil changes are available across all our weekend routes: Feltham, Bedfont, Ashford, Sunbury, Kingston, Hounslow, Twickenham, Richmond, Hampton, Hampton Hill, Hampton Court, and Brentford.
