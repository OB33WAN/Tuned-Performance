Questions

Common Questions About the Program

How do I get my card and first stamp?

Register below with your name and vehicle details. When you attend your first qualifying booking, your physical stamp card is handed to you and stamp one is added right away. Every paid qualifying job of £50 or more after that earns another stamp.

What counts as a qualifying job?

Any paid service worth £50 or more qualifies. Oil changes, brakes, diagnostics, remaps, scratch repairs, trim fitment, battery replacement — all count. MOT pickups and general consultations do not qualify.

How does the 5-stamp free oil change work?

We cover the full labour and parts up to £80 in value. If your oil and filter exceed £80, you pay only the difference. Alternatively, choose 50% off a scratch repair or panel detail instead — your call at the time of redemption.

What does the 10-stamp free labour mean?

We cover up to 3 hours of labour on any qualifying service of your choice. Parts are always paid by the customer. Book whatever job you need — the labour hours are on us up to the cap.

How does the referral stamp work?

Refer a friend and they complete a paid qualifying booking of £50 or more, and you earn one bonus stamp. Just make sure your name is mentioned at the time of their booking. There is no limit on how many friends you can refer.

Does my existing booking history count?

Yes. Customers who have already used Tuned Performance can register and have their previous bookings reviewed from day one. The more you have already booked, the closer you may already be to a milestone.

Do stamps or cards expire?

Cards are valid for 12 months from the date of your first stamp. After 12 months, a fresh card is issued. Keeping up regular bookings is the best way to protect your progress.

Can I use rewards as cash or transfer them?

No. Rewards are non-transferable and have no cash value. Only one stamp is issued per qualifying invoice, even if multiple jobs are completed on the same visit. Rewards must be redeemed directly with Tuned Performance.

Program Terms & Conditions

  • 1 stamp per qualifying job. Minimum spend £50 per invoice. One stamp per invoice regardless of number of jobs completed.
  • 5-stamp reward: free oil change up to £80 parts value (customer pays difference above cap), or 50% off a scratch repair / panel detail at standard listed price.
  • 10-stamp reward: free labour up to 3 hours maximum. Parts always paid by customer. One redemption per completed 10-stamp card.
  • Referral bonus: 1 stamp awarded when referred customer completes a qualifying paid booking. Referral must be declared at time of referred customer’s booking.
  • Cards valid for 12 months from date of first stamp. Stamps and rewards have no cash value and are non-transferable.
  • Tuned Performance reserves the right to amend or withdraw the loyalty program with reasonable notice. All rewards subject to availability.
