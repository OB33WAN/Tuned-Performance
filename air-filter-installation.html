Air Filter Options and Types

Standard Engine Air Filter

Replacement filters for most common vehicles. Paper-based media captures dust and particles effectively. Typical cost £15–£35. Suitable for general maintenance and fuel economy.

Labour: £50 | Total: £65–£85

Performance Air Filter

High-flow filters designed to improve engine breathing. Reusable cotton or foam media. Typical cost £30–£70. Can provide slight torque and power improvement with cleaner sound.

Labour: £50 | Total: £80–£120

Cabin Air Filter

Replacement for vehicle climate control system. Captures pollen, dust, and odours. Typical cost £15–£40. Improves air quality and HVAC efficiency. Often overlooked in maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace my air filter?

Engine air filters typically last 12,000–15,000 miles or 12 months. In dusty conditions, replace more frequently. Cabin air filters last 12–15 months. We'll assess your filter at booking and recommend replacement if needed.

Will a performance filter improve my fuel economy?

Performance filters with higher airflow *can* slightly improve fuel economy by reducing engine strain. Most drivers see negligible fuel savings but appreciate improved engine response and sound. The benefit is typically better throttle response than MPG.

Can I supply my own filter?

Yes, we accept customer-supplied filters on air filter jobs. Bring the filter to your appointment, and we'll inspect it for quality and compatibility before fitting. Labour cost remains £50.

Do you fit air intake kits?

Yes, we install full performance air intake systems including filter, tubing, and cold-air boxes. These are more involved than simple filter replacement and typically take 45–60 minutes. Cost depends on kit complexity (£50–£100 labour).

What if my filter doesn't fit my vehicle?

We'll assess compatibility at booking before any work. If a standard filter won't fit or isn't available, we'll source the correct part from Euro Car Parts, Autodoc, or GSF and fit it at your appointment.

Do you service all vehicle makes and models?

Yes, we fit air filters to all cars, vans, and light commercial vehicles. Most filters are universal in design; we'll confirm compatibility when you book.
