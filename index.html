Mobile Mechanic and Car Diagnostics FAQs

What should I send for the fastest quote?

Send your vehicle, postcode, service needed, preferred availability and clear photos if the job involves damage or fitment.

Where is Tuned Performance based?

Tuned Performance is based at 105 Swan Road, Feltham, TW13 6PE and handles mobile enquiries across Feltham, Bedfont, Ashford, Sunbury, Hounslow and Kingston.

What if the job needs a workshop?

Tuned Performance can route suitable jobs through trusted Feltham partner garages instead of making you restart the enquiry elsewhere.

Are weekday and weekend bookings available?

You can request weekday daytime, weekday evening, Saturday or Sunday availability. Final windows depend on route, job type and confirmation.

Can you code BMW or MINI hidden features?

Yes. BMW and MINI coding is available for supported BimmerCode-compatible vehicles. Send the model, year and feature list first so compatibility can be checked.

Should I book diagnostics before replacing parts?

If the car has a warning light, starting issue or running fault, diagnostics should usually come before parts ordering. Send the symptoms and any warning light photo first.
