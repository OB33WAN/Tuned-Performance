Fast answers for local drivers

What should I send for the fastest quote?

Send your vehicle, postcode, service needed, preferred availability and clear photos if the job involves damage or fitment.

What if the job needs a workshop?

Tuned Performance can route suitable jobs through trusted Feltham partner garages instead of making you restart the enquiry elsewhere.

Are weekday and weekend bookings available?

You can request weekday daytime, weekday evening, Saturday or Sunday availability. Final windows depend on route, job type and confirmation.

Can I enquire about ECU remapping now?

Yes. Remapping is enquiry-only at the moment, so the best next step is to register interest with make, model, engine and your goal.
