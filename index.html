FAQs

Common Questions Before Booking

Quick answers to the questions most people ask first.

Which areas do you cover?

Feltham, Bedfont, Hounslow, Twickenham, Richmond, Hampton, Hampton Hill, Hampton Court, Brentford, Ashford, Sunbury-on-Thames, and Kingston upon Thames. Route confirmed at booking based on your postcode.

What services do you offer?

Scratch and bumper repairs, brake pads and discs, battery replacement, engine oil changes, OBD diagnostics, trim and styling fitment, undertray repair, small fix bundles, and MOT pickup and drop-off support.

Do I need to provide my own parts?

No. Parts are sourced from Euro Car Parts, Autodoc, or GSF Car Parts. You can supply your own but the 12-month wear guarantee only applies to parts we supply.

How do I get a price quickly?

Use the estimator, check the full price list, or send your reg, postcode, service needed, and photos if relevant — we confirm scope and price before work starts.
