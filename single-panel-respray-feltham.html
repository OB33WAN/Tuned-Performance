FAQ

Questions this page answers

Is a single panel repair the same as a bumper scuff repair?

No. Single panel repair and re-spray can involve more preparation, paint matching and finish work than a small bumper scuff.

Can you quote from one photo?

A close-up helps, but a full-panel photo is also needed to understand the repair size and panel shape.

Can I request weekend availability?

Yes. Saturday or Sunday route options can be requested, subject to service type, route and confirmation.
