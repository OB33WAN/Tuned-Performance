Engine Oil Change

Mobile oil service — labour is flat rate, oil and filter priced separately based on your vehicle.

Oil Change (Filter Optional)

  • Labour — £35 (drain, refill & top-up check)
  • Oil — you supply, or we supply at market rate per litre (fully synthetic available)
  • Filter — £8–£15 parts extra (changed only if easily accessible)
  • Sump washer & consumables — £2–£3

Filter accessibility is confirmed at booking. Some vehicles require bumper or radiator removal to reach the filter — in those cases we change oil only and advise on filter replacement. Full filter change is available at a partnered garage; we can recommend one.

Oil Top-Up Only

  • Minimum charge — £25 (covers labour & travel)
  • Oil — £8–£15/L (depending on synthetic grade)
  • Includes level check, top-up to correct mark, dipstick verification

Top-up does not include a filter change. Prices include a 12-month guarantee on work carried out with our supplied oil.

Brake Pad Replacement

Mobile brake pad fitting for front and rear axles. Labour is fixed; pads and discs priced to your vehicle spec.

Labour

  • Front or rear axle — £50–£65
  • Front + rear same visit — £85–£110

Parts (per axle unless stated)

  • Economy pads — £40–£70
  • Mid-range pads — £50–£100
  • Premium / performance pads — £80–£180+
  • Brake discs — £35–£80 per disc
  • Wear sensor (if needed) — £15–£35

Disc inspection included at no extra charge. If discs are worn we quote pads and discs together. Customer-supplied parts accepted — labour only from £50. Provide make, model, year, and engine at booking for exact part spec.

Battery Replacement

Mobile battery fitting for standard, AGM, and EFB types. AGM and EFB vehicles require OBD programming after fitment.

Labour

  • Standard replacement — £35–£45
  • AGM / EFB + OBD coding — £55–£75

Battery (typical wholesale)

  • Standard 12V — £55–£90
  • AGM start-stop — £120–£190
  • EFB start-stop — £90–£130
  • Premium brand (BMW, Audi) — £160–£240
  • Old battery disposal — included

OBD programming is required for AGM and EFB vehicles. Confirm at booking by providing make, model, year, and engine size. BMW B-series engines typically require coding; older models may not.
