Exhaust Tip Questions

Frequently Asked About Exhaust Tips

Will exhaust tips fit my car?

Most vehicles have standard exhaust tailpipes. Tips fit over the existing tailpipe. We can confirm fitment compatibility before booking based on your vehicle model.

Do tips require welding or cutting?

No. Our tips are fitted by sliding over the existing tailpipe. No modifications to your exhaust system are needed. Completely reversible.

Will exhaust tips damage my car's warranty?

No. Since tips don't modify the exhaust system, they won't affect your warranty. They're cosmetic additions only.

How long do tips stay fitted?

Tips are fitted securely and will last for years. Stainless steel tips are more corrosion-resistant in wet climates. Chrome may tarnish over time but remains durable.

Can I fit dual exhaust tips?

Yes, if your vehicle has dual tailpipes. We can fit matching tips to both sides for a premium appearance.

Do you cover all 12 areas?

Yes. Exhaust tip fitment is available across all our weekend routes: Feltham, Bedfont, Ashford, Sunbury, Kingston, Hounslow, Twickenham, Richmond, Hampton, Hampton Hill, Hampton Court, and Brentford.
