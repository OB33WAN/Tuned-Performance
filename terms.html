Quotes and estimates

Website prices and estimator results are guide prices only. A final quote depends on inspection, photos, vehicle condition, access, parts, location and the scope of work. Tuned Performance may change or withdraw a quote if the job is different from the information provided.

Bookings and availability

Appointments are only confirmed once Tuned Performance agrees the service, price or pricing route, location, time window and any required parts or preparation. Weekday and weekend requests are subject to route planning and job suitability.

Customer responsibilities

Customers should provide accurate vehicle information, safe access to the vehicle, permission to work on the vehicle, and clear photos or part details where required. If customer-supplied parts are faulty, incorrect or incomplete, extra labour or a rebooking may be needed.

Payments, cancellations and rebooking

Payment terms are agreed before the job starts. Deposits may be requested for parts, longer bookings or sourced items. If weather, unsafe working conditions, unavailable parts or inaccurate job information prevent work from being completed, the appointment may need to be rebooked.

Cancellations and urgent work

Customers should contact Tuned Performance as soon as possible if they need to rearrange or cancel a confirmed booking. Where a customer asks for work to start urgently, or agrees that parts, paint or materials should be ordered for their vehicle, cancellation and refund options may be affected by costs already incurred. This does not affect statutory rights.

Service limitations

Mobile work is subject to weather, access, lighting, safety and vehicle condition. Diagnostics provide guidance based on the information available at the time and do not guarantee that every fault can be identified without further testing. ECU remapping is currently enquiry-only and not an instant-book service.

Nothing in these terms affects your statutory consumer rights.

Questions or complaints

If you are unhappy with a quote, booking or completed job, contact Tuned Performance as soon as possible with the vehicle details, booking information, photos where relevant and a clear description of the issue so it can be reviewed.

Website use

Website content is provided for general information and may change without notice. Do not copy website text, branding or assets without permission. External links, including WhatsApp, are controlled by their own providers and terms.
