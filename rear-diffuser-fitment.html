Diffuser Questions

Frequently Asked About Rear Diffuser Fitment

Will a diffuser fit my car?

Most vehicles have standard rear bumpers. We can confirm fitment compatibility based on your vehicle model and bumper type before booking.

Does fitment require drilling or cutting?

No. Our no-drill fitment uses industrial adhesive and clips. No modifications to your vehicle are needed. Completely reversible if desired.

How long does a diffuser last?

With proper installation using quality adhesive, a diffuser typically lasts 5+ years. ABS plastic resists UV and weather well. Carbon fiber lasts even longer.

Can I combine diffuser with mirror caps?

Yes. Many customers add mirror caps at the same time. Mirror caps take 20-30 minutes and cost from £25 + labour.

Will a diffuser affect my car's warranty?

No. Since fitment doesn't modify vehicle systems, it won't affect your manufacturer warranty. It's a cosmetic addition only.

Do you cover all 12 areas?

Yes. Diffuser fitment is available across all our weekend routes: Feltham, Bedfont, Ashford, Sunbury, Kingston, Hounslow, Twickenham, Richmond, Hampton, Hampton Hill, Hampton Court, and Brentford.
