What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of data stored on your device. They can remember choices, support website functions or help website owners understand how a site is used.

What this site uses

This static website does not set advertising cookies. It uses essential local storage called tp_cookie_choice to remember your cookie choice. If you choose Accept analytics, Google Analytics measurement ID G-Z6M09GYPFY loads to measure page views and conversion clicks such as calls, WhatsApp clicks, enquiry submissions and review link clicks.

Analytics consent

Analytics is optional. Choosing Essential only keeps the site usable and prevents the Google Analytics tag from loading on future page views. Choosing Accept analytics allows Google Analytics to measure website activity and conversion actions.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Google reviews, Trustpilot, phone providers or future social platforms may open external services. Those services may set their own cookies or process data under their own policies once you leave this website. The Trustpilot widget is embedded on the reviews page and may load Trustpilot resources when that page is opened.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. You can also reset the stored choice below and choose again from the banner.
