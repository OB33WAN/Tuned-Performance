Cookie policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026. This page explains what this static website stores and how optional analytics and external services may use cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of data stored on your device. They can remember choices, support website functions or help website owners understand how a site is used.

What this site uses

This static website does not set advertising cookies. It uses essential session storage called tp_cookie_choice to remember your cookie choice while you move between pages in the current browser session. The choice expires when that session ends. If you choose Accept analytics, Google Analytics measurement ID G-Z6M09GYPFY loads to measure page views and conversion clicks such as calls, WhatsApp clicks, enquiry submissions and review link clicks.

Analytics consent

Analytics is optional. Choosing Essential only keeps the site usable and prevents the Google Analytics tag from loading on later page views in the current session. Choosing Accept analytics allows Google Analytics to measure website activity and conversion actions.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Google reviews, Trustpilot, BimmerCode, GOV.UK, phone providers or future social platforms may open external services. Those services may set their own cookies or process data under their own policies once you leave this website. The Trustpilot widget is embedded on the reviews page and may load Trustpilot resources when that page is opened. Web3Forms handles submitted email enquiry data when a form is sent.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and session storage in your browser settings. Your choice also expires when the current browser session ends. You can also reset the stored choice below and choose again from the banner.
