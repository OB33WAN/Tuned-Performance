MOT Warning Light FAQs

Can a warning light fail an MOT?

Some warning lights can affect MOT outcome depending on the system and vehicle. Use GOV.UK MOT information and send the warning details for guidance.

Should I book diagnostics before MOT?

If a warning light is active, diagnostics can help identify the next step before the MOT or after a fail.

Can you arrange MOT support?

Tuned Performance can help with MOT support enquiries and local routing where suitable.

Need MOT-related diagnostic guidance?
